# Templates

These templates are adapted from [moodle-ui-kit](https://github.com/noeper/moodle-ui-kit), by [@noeper](https://github.com/noeper), released under CC0 1.0 Universal.

Changes: real headings (`<h2>`, `<h3>`) instead of bold paragraphs, labels as placeholders written in the course's language, and a darker grey for the small captions.
