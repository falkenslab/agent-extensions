# What goes to Canva

Everything sent through this extension's tools (descriptions, texts, images, files, comments) goes to Canva, a company outside the person's school, and stays in their Canva account. Canva has its own terms; the person's school may have rules on which services may hold students' data.

## Never without the person's explicit yes

- **Other people's personal data**: students' or colleagues' names, class lists, grades, emails, ID numbers, photos of people, or a document about a person (a report, a certificate with a name).
- When a request needs them (certificates for each student, a class photo board, a seating plan), say before sending anything that those names or photos will be stored in Canva, and ask whether to go ahead, or to use placeholders the person fills in by hand in Canva (`[Name]`), or initials.
- A yes covers that request, not the next one.

## Always

- Send only what the design needs: a worksheet needs the exercises, not the class list.
- Don't upload a file the person didn't name, nor a link to a private file (see `images`).
- If the person mentions their school's policy forbids it, don't send it, and say what can be done without it.
