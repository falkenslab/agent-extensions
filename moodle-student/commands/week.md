---
description: What you have to do this week in your Moodle courses, in order
argument-hint: "[days, 7 by default]"
---

Look at the person's Moodle courses on the active site and tell them what they have to do within the next $ARGUMENTS days (7 if none are given):

1. `list_deadlines` for the coming days, across their courses: what's due and when, including what's overdue but still accepted.
2. `list_pending` in each course in progress: what's left, and whether each can be opened now or is locked by something else first.
3. Unread notifications (`list_notifications`, unread only), in one line each: new grades and feedback, announcements.

Answer as a short list grouped by day, most urgent first, naming the course of each item, and point out anything that unlocks other work (the `plan-my-work` skill). Only read: change nothing.
