---
description: What's due this week in your Moodle courses, and what's waiting for you
argument-hint: "[days, 7 by default]"
---

Look at the person's Moodle courses on the active site and tell them what this week holds, within the next $ARGUMENTS days (7 if none are given):

1. `list_deadlines` for the coming days, across their courses: what's due and when.
2. As a teacher, what's waiting for them: submissions to grade (`list_assignments`, then `get_submissions` for the ones past due) and quiz questions to grade by hand (`list_questions_to_grade`). As a student, `list_pending` per course.
3. Unread notifications (`list_notifications`, unread only), in one line each.

Answer as a short list grouped by day, most urgent first, naming the course of each item. Only read: change nothing.
